1. Getting started

Grubpac Attendance is your self-service portal for daily attendance, leave requests, balances, and pay estimates. Sign in on the Employee tab using your work email or employee code and password.

  1. Open the staging portal at d24p2zn8763d4h.cloudfront.net.
  2. Choose Employee on the login screen and enter your credentials.
  3. After login you land on the Dashboard — your home for check-in/out and the month calendar.
  4. Use the bottom navigation (mobile) or sidebar (desktop) for Leave, Attendance history, Help, and FAQ.
Tip: If you also have a manager or admin role, you may switch portals from your profile. This guide covers the employee experience only.

2. Daily tasks — check in & check out

No Office / WFH toggle

You do not choose Office or WFH manually. Tap Check in or Check out only — the server automatically detects whether you are at the office (within the configured geofence) or working from home.

Office check-in

  1. Allow location access when prompted (required for attendance).
  2. On the Dashboard, confirm your location shows you are within the office radius.
  3. Tap Check in when you start work and Check out when you leave.

WFH check-in

To work from home on a given day, you must have approved WFH leave for that date. Without it, check-in from home will be blocked with a message that WFH approval is required.

Once WFH is approved, you may check in and check out from your home location as usual.

When check-in is blocked

  • Approved non-WFH leave (CL, EL, SL, etc.) for today — you cannot check in on a full leave day.
  • No approved WFH when you are outside the office geofence.
  • Outside office radius when the system expects an office check-in.
  • Already checked in/out for the current session.

3. Leave & work from home (WFH)

Applying for leave

Go to Apply leave / WFH from the Leave section. Select the leave type, date range, and reason.

Application deadlines

Leave typeDeadline for tomorrow
Sick Leave (SL) Can be applied anytime — no 11:59 PM cutoff for tomorrow.
All other types (CL, EL, WFH, CO, RH, etc.) Must be submitted before 11:59 PM IST today to include tomorrow.

After 11:59 PM IST, the portal will reject new requests that cover the next calendar day (IST).

WFH vs other leave

  • WFH — apply in advance; once approved, you may check in from home on those dates.
  • CL, EL, RH, etc. — approved days block check-in; the day counts as leave.
  • SL — often auto-approved; still blocks check-in on approved sick days.
Permanent / long-term WFH: The portal does not have a separate “permanent WFH” mode. For extended work-from-home arrangements, submit a long WFH leave request for approval, or ask HR/Admin to configure your account appropriately.

4. Calendar & approved leave

The month calendar on your Dashboard highlights days with approved leave so you can see your schedule at a glance. Pending requests may show differently from approved leave — check My requests for status.

Use Leave balances to see remaining quota for each leave type.

5. Attendance history & outcome codes

Open Attendance history to review past check-ins. Each day shows short OUTCOME and MODE codes:

CodeMeaning
PPresent — on-time office check-in.
OFCOffice mode — checked in at the office.
WFHWork from home — approved WFH day.
HDHalf day — check-in at or after the half-day threshold.
W1, W2, W3Late present with quarterly warning (1st, 2nd, or 3rd warning).
LVLeave violation — late check-in with no warnings remaining in the quarter.

Check-out rows inherit the same outcome code as that day’s check-in.

6. Troubleshooting

Location not detected

  • Enable GPS/location for your browser.
  • On mobile, allow location “While using the app”.
  • Refresh the page and wait a few seconds for the map to update.

Cannot check in at office

  • Move closer until you are inside the office geofence (shown on the map).
  • Confirm you do not have approved leave for today.

Cannot check in from home

  • Ensure WFH leave is approved for today (not just pending).
  • Apply WFH before the 11:59 PM IST cutoff if the date is tomorrow.

Still stuck?

Raise a Help ticket (category: Attendance or Leave) with a screenshot if possible.

7. FAQ

Do I need to select Office or WFH?

No. The system detects your location and approved leave automatically.

Can I check in on a casual leave day?

No. Approved CL/EL/SL (non-WFH) leave blocks check-in for that day.

Why was my tomorrow WFH request rejected?

Non-SL leave for tomorrow must be submitted before 11:59 PM IST today.

I work from home every day. What should I do?

There is no permanent WFH toggle. Request long-duration WFH approval or contact HR/Admin for setup.

Where is my pay information?

My pay estimate (if enabled for your role) shows a monthly estimate — not a payslip.
