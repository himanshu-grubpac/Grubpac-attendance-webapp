1. Getting started

As a Reporting Manager, you use the Admin / Management portal to oversee direct reports — leave approvals, team attendance, and help tickets. You may also switch to the Employee portal for your own check-in/out.

  1. Sign in at d24p2zn8763d4h.cloudfront.net on the Admin tab (or Employee tab for self-service).
  2. Your default landing page is the Dashboard with team summary widgets.
  3. Key menu items: Pending Requests, Attendance history, Team issues (help).
  4. If you manage one or more departments (Department Head), also read the Department Head User Guide.
Scope: Reporting managers approve leave and view attendance for employees who list you as their reporting manager. Department-wide scope requires the Department Head guide.

2. Your team's attendance (what employees see)

Train your team on current attendance rules:

  • No Office/WFH toggle — employees tap Check in/out only; the server auto-detects location.
  • WFH requires approved WFH leave for that day; home check-in/out is then allowed.
  • Approved non-WFH leave (CL, EL, SL, etc.) blocks check-in for that day.
  • Apply deadline: SL anytime; all other types must be submitted before 11:59 PM IST to include tomorrow.
  • Calendar on the employee dashboard highlights approved leave days.
  • No permanent WFH in the system — long WFH needs approval or admin setup.

3. Leave & WFH approvals

  1. Open Pending Requests from the sidebar or bottom nav.
  2. Review requester, leave type, dates, and reason.
  3. Approve or Reject with an optional comment.
  4. WFH approvals enable home check-in on approved dates only.

SL (Sick Leave)

Sick leave is often auto-approved by policy. You may still see SL requests in edge cases — treat urgent medical leave promptly.

Deadline awareness

If a direct report could not apply CL/WFH for tomorrow after 11:59 PM IST, they must contact HR/Admin for a manual adjustment — you cannot override the cutoff in the portal.

4. Team visibility

Attendance history

Attendance history (admin portal) shows check-in/out for your direct reports. Filter by employee and date range. Use outcome codes to spot late patterns (W1–LV).

Late Warning

If enabled, Late Warning shows quarterly late-check-in warnings for your team scope.

Employee list

Employee List is read-only for managers — view profiles and reporting lines, not full HR edits.

5. Help tickets

Team issues lists help tickets raised by your direct reports.

  1. Open a ticket to read the description and attachments.
  2. Add comments to request more information or confirm resolution.
  3. Update status (e.g. In progress → Resolved) when done.

Company-wide IT/HR tickets are handled by admins via Help tickets (full admin only).

6. Troubleshooting

Employee cannot check in from home

  • Confirm WFH leave is approved for today.
  • Pending WFH does not allow check-in.

Leave request not in my queue

  • Verify you are set as their reporting manager on their profile.
  • Department members outside your direct reports may route to their own manager or a dept head.

Cannot approve a request

You can only approve requests you are authorized for. Escalate to HR/Admin if routing is wrong.

7. FAQ

Reporting Manager vs Department Head?

Reporting Manager: direct reports only.
Department Head: reporting manager with managed departments — broader team and leave queue visibility.

Can I check in for my team?

No. Each employee checks in for themselves. Admins may correct records in exceptional cases.

Do I use Admin or Employee login?

Use Admin for team tasks; switch to Employee for your own attendance.
