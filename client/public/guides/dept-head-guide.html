1. Getting started

A Department Head is a reporting manager assigned to one or more managed departments in the system. You use the same Admin portal as other managers, with expanded visibility across everyone in those departments — not only your direct reports.

  1. Sign in on the Admin tab at d24p2zn8763d4h.cloudfront.net.
  2. Confirm your profile lists the correct managed departments (HR/Admin sets this).
  3. Use Pending Requests, Attendance history, and Team issues for day-to-day oversight.
  4. Also keep the Reporting Manager Guide handy for direct-report workflows.

3. Attendance rules for your department

Ensure your team understands current policy:

  • Check in/out only — no manual Office/WFH selection; location + approved leave drive mode.
  • WFH days need approved WFH leave; then home check-in/out works.
  • CL, EL, SL, RH, etc. — approved days block check-in.
  • SL — apply anytime; other types for tomorrow before 11:59 PM IST today.
  • Dashboard calendar highlights approved leave.
  • Permanent WFH is not a system mode — use long WFH approval or ask Admin/HR.

Outcome codes

P, OFC, WFH, HD, W1–W3, LV — see the Employee or Reporting Manager guide for definitions.

4. Leave & WFH approvals

  1. Monitor Pending Requests daily — especially WFH for next-day planning.
  2. Approve WFH only when policy allows; rejected WFH means the employee must be at office to check in.
  3. Watch for multi-day WFH patterns that may need HR discussion (no permanent WFH flag exists).
  4. After 11:59 PM IST, non-SL requests for tomorrow cannot be submitted by employees — escalate to HR if exceptions are needed.

6. Troubleshooting

Employee visible in queue but I cannot approve

They may report to another manager. Visibility ≠ approval rights. Reassign reporting manager in HR if needed.

Whole team blocked from WFH check-in

Confirm WFH requests are approved for the date, not pending.

Department not in my managed list

Ask Admin/HR to update managed departments on your employee profile.

7. FAQ

Am I also a Reporting Manager?

Yes — department heads are reporting managers with extra department scope. Use both guides as needed.

Can I change leave policies?

No — that requires HR/Admin with Leave policies permission.

Can I register new employees?

Typically HR/Admin only. You can view the employee list if your role includes user read access.
